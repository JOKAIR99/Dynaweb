# Code of Conduct

Dynaweb adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. It applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other channel where you represent the project.

## In short

- Be welcoming and respectful to people of every background and experience level.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Critique work, not people.
- Do not harass, insult, demean, or make unwelcome sexual or personal comments.
- Do not publish other people's private information without their explicit permission.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, @JOKAIR99, through the contact on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/JOKAIR99). Do not report it in a public issue. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews every report and may respond with a correction, a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban, following the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
